Section 11

Conclusion

Key takeaways, open questions, complete reference list with BibTeX export.

Key Takeaways

Finding 1: CNN RF is Severely Limited
With 4 layers and kernel size 3, the theoretical RF is just 9 px. Effective RF (gradient backprop) is even smaller. This makes CNN fundamentally unsuitable for tasks requiring global context, such as flood mapping or land-use classification.
Finding 2: ViT Achieves Global RF at Cost of Quadratic Complexity
Every ViT layer has a global RF — even layer 1. However, this comes with $O(N^2)$ memory. For remote sensing at high resolution ($1024^2$ with patch 16: $N=4096$), a single attention layer requires 64M attention values per head — prohibitive on most hardware.
Finding 3: Mamba Bridges the Gap
Mamba achieves $O(N)$ complexity with causal global context. The IIR filter structure provides richer frequency response than CNN's FIR. The HiPPO-inspired initialisation gives diverse memory timescales. The main limitation is the causal 1D scan, which breaks 2D spatial symmetry.
Finding 4: Architecture Choice Should Be Task-Driven
No single architecture dominates across all remote sensing tasks. CNN for data-efficient, texture-based tasks (SAR, road extraction). ViT when global context matters and data is abundant or pretraining is available. Mamba for sequential/spectral data or high-resolution inference.
Finding 5: All Architectures are Instances of a Unified SSM
CNN = FIR SSM (time-invariant, finite filter). Mamba = IIR SSM (time-varying via selectivity, infinite filter). ViT = data-adaptive non-parametric kernel regression. The SSM lens reveals why Mamba has strictly more expressive power than CNN in the frequency domain.

Open Questions

  • Can a bidirectional Mamba scan (Vim: Zhu et al. 2024) recover the 2D symmetry needed for remote sensing?
  • How do ERF shapes change with dilation, depth-wise convolutions, or dilated Mamba state matrices?
  • Does the low-pass bias of Mamba help or hurt in high-frequency SAR speckle interpretation?
  • Can we design a hybrid architecture with CNN's local texture, ViT's global attention, and Mamba's linear complexity?
