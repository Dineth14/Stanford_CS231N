Case Studies

Case Study 1: ViT Fails on SAR Without Pretraining

⚠ The SAR Generalisation Gap
SAR imagery is dominated by speckle noise (high-frequency multiplicative noise). ViT, with no locality prior, attends uniformly at initialisation. Without ImageNet pretraining, it cannot learn the high-frequency speckle statistics of SAR — it needs far more data. CNN's inherent high-frequency sensitivity (texture bias) means it can learn SAR statistics from relatively small datasets.

Case Study 2: CNN Fails on Large-Area Flood Mapping

⚠ The Receptive Field Bottleneck
Flood extents span thousands of pixels. A CNN with RF=9 on a 1024×1024 image sees 0.9% of the scene per neuron. It can detect water locally (correct spectral signature) but cannot integrate global context to understand flood extent or connectivity. ViT or Mamba's long-range dependencies are essential here.

Case Study 3: Mamba for Hyperspectral Sequences

✓ The Spectral Sequence Advantage
Hyperspectral images have 100–400 spectral bands that must be jointly interpreted. Treating bands as a sequence (L=200 tokens) exploits Mamba's linear $O(N)$ complexity and the SSM's natural long-range memory. The IIR filter structure is particularly suited to the smooth spectral correlations in hyperspectral data.
