Training Dynamics

Loss curves, gradient norms, and weight update magnitudes from the toy training experiment (500 images, 20 epochs, random labels).

Experiment Setup

ℹ️ Toy Experiment Note
500 random images, random labels, 10 classes. The purpose is NOT to measure accuracy — it's to observe convergence behaviour, gradient flow, and optimisation dynamics that reveal architectural characteristics. Adam lr=1e-3, 20 epochs.

Training Loss Curves

Cross-Entropy Loss per Epoch
CNN typically converges fastest (inductive bias helps even with random labels). ViT starts slowly (no spatial prior → explores more). Mamba is smooth (stable SSM eigenvalues).

Gradient Norms

Gradient L2 Norm per Epoch
Exploding gradients → norm grows unboundedly. Vanishing gradients → norm collapses to 0. BatchNorm helps CNN stay stable. ViT LayerNorm + gradient clipping is typically needed. Mamba benefits from stable negative eigenvalues in A.

Gradient Flow Theory

For a network with $L$ layers, the gradient at layer $l$ is:

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W_l} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h_L} \prod_{k=l+1}^{L} \frac{\partial h_k}{\partial h_{k-1}} \cdot \frac{\partial h_l}{\partial W_l}$$

If the Jacobian norm $\|\partial h_k / \partial h_{k-1}\| < 1$, gradients vanish exponentially. If $> 1$, they explode. BatchNorm and residual connections help maintain norms near 1.

SSM's gradient through the recurrence $h_t = \bar{A}h_{t-1} + \bar{B}u_t$ has gradient: $\partial h_t/\partial h_{t-1} = \bar{A}$. Since all diagonal elements of $\bar{A} \in (0,1)$, gradients are bounded without additional tricks.

Weight Update Magnitude

Mean Weight Update Magnitude per Epoch
Mean L2 norm of weight deltas across all trainable layers.

CNN

Fast convergence. Early large updates, then stabilise as the model learns local features. Inductive bias guides the optimiser efficiently.

ViT

Needs learning rate warmup. Without pretraining, attention weights are initially near-uniform (all tokens contribute equally), causing slow convergence.

Mamba

Smooth gradient flow from bounded $\bar{A}$. The gating mechanism (SiLU) provides adaptive learning rate effects. Generally stable updates.
